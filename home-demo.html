The basics

Language is not a subject. It's a skill.

We only get better at skills by practicing them.

If you want to turn passive vocabulary into active vocabularySpeak often on a variety of topics.
If you want to sound more naturalDon't overcomplicate it. Copy native patterns.
If you want to fix habitual mistakesCatch them and correct them in the moment.
If you want to get comfortable making small talkPractice small talk regularly.

You don't need more understanding and analysis. You need active listening and a regular speaking habit.
